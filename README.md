# Innovation-economics PhD schools & workshops — dates and deadlines

A one-page calendar of recurring summer/winter schools and early-career workshops in the economics of innovation, science and IP. Built for PhD students planning a year ahead.

**Last verified: 26 September 2026.** Every row links to the organiser's own page for the latest edition with a published call, and quotes the deadline exactly as stated there. "Next edition" says what was (not yet) announced at verification time. Open a PR when a new call is out.

## Events (by month of the event)

### January — DRUID Academy
Early-career conference (PhD students, postdocs, assistant professors ≤2 years post-PhD). One-page abstract; junior + senior discussant per paper; Bent Dalum PhD Award. Fee €300 incl. meals.
- **Latest edition:** DRUID Academy 2026, Aarhus University, 21–23 January 2026
- **Deadlines (2026 edition):** submission site opened 6 Oct 2025 · **abstract deadline 17 November 2025, before midnight, your local time** · full paper 12 Dec 2025 · registration & payment 15 Dec 2025
- **Next edition:** 2027 call not yet published as of 26 Sep 2026 — pattern is opening early Oct, deadline mid-Nov (2024: 12 Nov; 2025: 18 Nov)
- **Links:** [Academy 2026 page](https://conference.druid.dk/Druid/?confId=71) · [timeline & guidelines](https://conference.druid.dk/Druid/infoSite.xhtml?itemId=503) · [druid.dk](https://www.druid.dk) · submissions via conference.druid.dk (create profile)

### January–February — Geography of Innovation Winter School (Pavia)
Univ. of Pavia (Dept. of Political and Social Sciences) + ICRIOS-Bocconi. 5 days; ~10–12 speakers; two data-lab sessions (R/Python: complexity, diversification, networks); participants present and meet journal editors. Fee €250. Rolling admission, limited places.
- **Latest edition:** 2–6 February 2026, University of Pavia
- **Deadline (2026 edition):** **15 October 2025** (2025 edition, 27–31 Jan 2025: 24 Nov 2024)
- **Next edition:** 2027 not announced as of 26 Sep 2026 — **the 2026 deadline fell mid-October, so check now**
- **Links:** [2026 call (RSA listing)](https://www.regionalstudies.org/opportunities/winter-school-geography-of-innovation-university-of-pavia-2-6-february-2026) · registration: https://tinyurl.com/paviawinterschool2026 · [ICRIOS winter-school page](https://icrios.unibocconi.eu/geography-innovation/winter-school)

### May — Lake Como School: Summer School in Sustainable Entrepreneurship, Innovation and Entrepreneurial Finance (SEIF)
Organised by Univ. Milano-Bicocca, Bocconi, Politecnico di Milano and Univ. of Milan; hosted by the Lake Como School of Advanced Studies, Villa del Grumello, Como. Lectures, paper-development sessions, practitioner roundtable. Max 15 participants (3rd ed.).
- **Latest edition:** 3rd edition, 4–6 May 2026
- **Deadline (2026 edition):** **5 March 2026** (2025: 14 Feb 2025; 2024: 31 Jan 2024)
- **Next edition:** 2027 call expected Jan–Feb 2027
- **Links:** [seif3.lakecomoschool.org](https://seif3.lakecomoschool.org/) · [online application](https://seif3.lakecomoschool.org/application/) · [call for participation (PDF)](https://seif3.lakecomoschool.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/73/2026/03/SEIF-Summer-school_Call-for-participants_2026.pdf) · scientific queries: musasummerschool@unimib.it

- **Deadlines (2026 edition):** **submit by 8 May 2026 to sti@kuleuven.be** · notification 22 May 2026 (2025 Leuven edition: 23 June 2025; 2024 Antwerp: 1 June, extended 15 June)

### September (varies) — Bessemer Symposium (Early Careers Research Symposium)

- **Latest edition with a public call:** "Bessemer Symposium in Economic Geography of Innovation", 19–20 September 2024, Sheffield
- **Deadline (2024 edition):** **Sunday 4 August 2024 (close of play)**
- **Next edition:** no later public call located as of 26 Sep 2026; a further edition ("Economics of Innovation") was reported in April 2026 — timing not fixed, watch IRC news
- **Links:** [2024 call](https://ircaucus.ac.uk/call-for-papers-for-the-bessemer-symposium-in-economic-geography-of-innovation-early-careers-research-symposium/) · [IRC news & updates](https://ircaucus.ac.uk/news-and-updates/)

### September–October — Competition and Innovation Summer School (CISS)
Since 2009; ZEW, DICE Düsseldorf, KU Leuven, Univ. of Luxembourg, EPFL et al. Morning lectures, student workshops with faculty discussants, research-methods workshops. Fee €350 incl. single room, full board, airport shuttle.
- **Latest edition:** 21–25 September 2026, Ulcinj, Montenegro
- **Deadline (2026 edition):** **30 June 2026** (extended abstract or full paper) (2024 edition, 30 Sep–4 Oct: 12 Aug 2024)
- **Next edition:** 2027 call expected ~May 2027
- **Links:** [euro-ciss.eu](https://euro-ciss.eu/) · [apply](https://euro-ciss.eu/apply/) · [CfP 2026 (PDF)](https://euro-ciss.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/CfP_CISS-2026.pdf) · info@euro-ciss.eu

### December — RISE Workshop (Research on Innovation, Science and Entrepreneurship)
Max Planck Institute for Innovation and Competition, Munich; organised by the PhD students/postdocs of Dietmar Harhoff's department. Keynote + ~10 empirical papers, each with a senior discussant (40 min per paper). Travel grants available on request.
- **Latest edition:** RISE9, 14–15 December 2026, Munich
- **Deadline (2026 edition):** **24 July 2026** (full paper or ≥3-page extended abstract, online tool) (RISE7 2024: 26 Jul; registration 13 Sep; full paper 25 Nov)
- **Next edition:** 2027 call expected ~May 2027
- **Links:** [RISE workshop page](https://www.ip.mpg.de/en/research/innovation-and-entrepreneurship-research/rise-workshop.html) · [RISE9 call (PDF)](https://www.ip.mpg.de/fileadmin/ipmpg/content/forschung/Call_for_Papers_RISE9.pdf) · [RISE9 event site](https://plan.events.mpg.de/e/rise9) · [news item](https://www.ip.mpg.de/en/the-institute/institute-news/call-for-papers-rise9-workshop.html) · rise_workshop@ip.mpg.de

### December — WINGS Workshop (Economics of INnovation, Geography and Science)
Successor of WICK (Workshop in Economics of Innovation, Complexity and Knowledge, 12 editions to 2024). Jointly organised by the PhD in Innovation for the Circular Economy (Univ. of Turin), the Vilfredo Pareto Doctoral Program (Univ. of Turin & Collegio Carlo Alberto) and YSI. Apply with an extended abstract (max 1,000 words); full paper or advanced presentation due before the workshop; every participant presents.
- **Latest edition:** WINGS#1, 11–12 December 2025, Turin
- **Deadline (2025 edition):** **27 October 2025**
- **Next edition:** WINGS#2 (Dec 2026) call not found as of 26 Sep 2026 — **expect an October 2026 deadline; check now**
- **Links:** WINGS site: https://sites.google.com/view/wingsworkshop · [SIE announcement](https://www.siecon.org/en/node/7858) · [WICK legacy site](https://wick.carloalberto.org)
